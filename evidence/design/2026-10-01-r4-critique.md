# Paperloft Receipts: design critique, fourth review (AC-16), 2026-10-01

Reviewer: critic (Mac design lead role). Reviewed against the macOS Human Interface Guidelines and the six-line rubric. Earlier reviews: `evidence/design/2026-09-30-critique.md`, `2026-09-30-r2-critique.md` and `2026-10-01-critique.md` (third review). I scored what I saw today, independently of the earlier scores.

Build: QA build `build/QADerivedData/Build/Products/QA/Paperloft Receipts.app` (bundle id `app.paperloft.receipts`, version 1.1 (1), worktree `paperloft-appreview`, branch `local/app-review-fixes`, commit `f792268`, not rebuilt).

Method: I looked only at the running app, with the background computer-use tools (screenshots, accessibility tree, clicks, background keystrokes, menu commands). I read no source code or tests. GUI lock `~/Factory/.gui.lock` was held as `critic4` from 01:49Z until 02:12Z (2 October UTC), then removed. The app was quit at the end of each launch. The Mac's appearance is Light. I switched appearance with the app's own Settings › General › Theme control and left it on **System** in all three profiles. Contrast figures come from pixel samples of the saved screenshots (WCAG formula).

Launches:

- **A (first launch):** `-PaperloftQAProfile critic4-onboarding -PaperloftStoreMock Free -PaperloftScreenshotMode YES`. Onboarding, the sidebar Settings page, the Settings window, the paywall from Settings, Help, then Try with Samples. In the practice library I filed, undid, removed and restored receipts.
- **B (Pro):** `-PaperloftQAProfile critic4-pro -PaperloftStoreMock YES -PaperloftScreenshotMode YES`. Try with Samples, then I filed all five samples (menu commands), used the Library, search, Delete and Recently Deleted, the export sheet, History, Settings and the Pro paywall state.
- **C (limit):** `-PaperloftQAProfile critic4-limit -PaperloftStoreMock Free -PaperloftQuotaUsed 25 -PaperloftScreenshotMode YES`. Try with Samples, then I sent a plain PDF I made in my scratchpad (`birch-hardware-r4.pdf`) with `open -a`. That showed the automatic paywall, the limit pane, Fill In Details and the export gate.
- **Purchases and sign-in:** I pressed no purchase button. No system purchase sheet or sign-in sheet appeared. I entered no password, Apple ID or payment detail.
- **Menu bar extra:** the status item isn't reachable with the background tools (they can't click the menu bar's status area). As agreed, I scored it from `evidence/design/2026-10-01-menubar/menu-bar-extra-light.png` and `-dark.png` (UI-test captures from this build's code).

Scores: 5 excellent, 4 good, 3 needs work, 2 poor, 1 broken. Every score below 4 has a fix.

## 1. Top findings

Most of what the third review asked for is fixed, and fixed well. The paywall is now a proper Mac sheet with a reason-specific headline, a radio plan picker, one default button whose title follows the plan, Not Now, a Pro state, and messages that clear when it closes. The review pane no longer narrows after a filing, and the window no longer overflows. Rows have one shape. Undo of a filing brings the receipt back exactly, with no re-read. The limit pane shows the document and the reset date. Settings shows quota progress and the reset date, and the About block is gone. The export sheet refuses an empty period and says why. The Library says "receipts" throughout. Every button uses a real ellipsis. Of the six screens the third review scored, lines below 4 go from 21 to 7.

What still needs work, ranked by severity:

1. **P1: One Edit › Undo can reverse two actions.** I filed Maple Desk Supply with the Confirm button, then removed Juniper Cafe with the Remove button, then chose Edit › Undo once. Both came back to the Inbox, and the notice named only the filing ("Returned 01-office.pdf to the Inbox"). I reproduced this twice (`32`; `35` → `36` → `37`). With menu commands (Receipt › Remove from Inbox, then Receipt › Confirm and File) each Undo reversed one action, as it should (`33`, `34`). I pressed the buttons through accessibility, which is the same path VoiceOver uses; I couldn't test real mouse clicks from the background. This silently undoes a filing the user didn't ask to undo.
   **Fix:** wrap each action's undo registration in its own group (`undoManager.beginUndoGrouping()` / `endUndoGrouping()`), or register it synchronously inside the button action rather than from the filing's async completion. Set action names so the menu reads "Undo File Receipt" and "Undo Remove from Inbox" (today it says only "Undo"). Make the notice describe what was undone: after undoing a removal it still showed the previous filing's text (`34`); it should say "Restored 04-software.pdf to the Inbox".
2. **P2: Dark-mode selection contrast is not fixed.** With the list focused, the selected row is white text on #66AA7B, **2.76:1** (`22`, `22b`), and **2.80:1** in the limit profile (`64`, `64b`), the same colour as in the third review. The badge on the row is harder still to read. The unfocused selection (#464646, 9.4:1, `21`) and the light-mode selection (#14361E, 13.3:1, `29`) are fine. I tested under Settings › Theme › Dark with the Mac in Light. If the darker colour keys off the system appearance, it doesn't reach the in-app Dark theme.
   **Fix:** use #2E6B47 for the focused selection fill in dark (6.3:1 with white), and resolve the colour from the window's effective appearance so the in-app theme gets it.
3. **P2: The notice banner covers the Issues chip.** At the default window width (1180 pt) the banner sits over the end of the chip row, so "Issues" and its count are hidden for as long as it shows (`24`, `24b`, `30`, `31`). In my background session it stayed for more than 45 seconds (`25`); I think the timer pauses while the app isn't frontmost, which is reasonable, but the chip stays covered for that whole time.
   **Fix:** move the notice out of the chip row. Overlay it at the bottom of the list column (`.overlay(alignment: .bottom)` with a 12 pt inset), where it covers nothing interactive, and keep the 8 s timer.
4. **P2: The button bar shows two layers at once after a filing.** For a moment after each filing, the text "Finishing a library change. Filing will be available when it completes." is drawn underneath Remove and Confirm (`24c`, `30b`).
   **Fix:** show that text in place of the hint line ("Return to confirm · Tab to move between fields") and disable Confirm with a small `ProgressView`, rather than cross-fading two views in the same place.
5. **P2: The row badge and the form disagree.** After Fill In Details, the row says "Check date and total", but the form outlines only Total and says "Verify total and receipt details." (`59`, `62`).
   **Fix:** flag the Date field too, and use the badge's words in the note: "Check the date and total against the receipt."
6. **P2: The review form's layout and number formatting.**
   - Total still shows "45.47" and "25.92" with no currency symbol. Use a currency-formatted field ("$45.47") with `.monospacedDigit()`.
   - When the window shrinks, the fields column gives up width before the preview does. After resizing from 1394 to 1180 pt, Vendor is 173 pt wide (208 pt at first launch) and the Total note wraps (`65`). Give the preview the lower holding priority, and keep the fields column at `minWidth: 320, idealWidth: 360`.
7. **P2: The menu bar extra's count is the faintest text in the panel.** "4 receipts waiting in your Inbox" is tertiary grey, 2.9:1 in light and 3.15:1 in dark, while "Paperloft" gets the bold title spot. Details in 2.7.
8. **P2: Help still uses old names.** It says "Accountant packs are part of Paperloft Pro" and "Export Accountant Pack … returns packs up to 100 MB" (`17`), while the menus, paywall and export sheet say "Tax & Accountant Export". Use one name everywhere.
9. **Owner decisions (PROPOSALS.md table) and the sidebar toggle.** The inline Settings page, the serif tagline band, the custom chips, the row checkboxes, the date text field and the Library card rows are unchanged. So is the grey circle behind the sidebar toggle, which isn't in the owner table. Section 2 says, line by line, whether the lead's recommendation would bring each line to 4.

Smaller items, all minor: the Delete alert for a restorable action (`43`); the preview disappears behind a spinner during Fill In Details (`58`); disabled Choose Watched Folder… and Turn On buttons still show on Free (`04`); the paywall moves 8 pt when the restore message appears (`09`); History shows both "Undone" and "Returned to the Inbox for review" (`38`); the Tab stop after Tax still has no visible focus (`28`); "Choose files…" and "Paste image" use sentence case while other buttons use title case (`54`, `55`).

**Icon:** unchanged since the third review. The QA build's `AppIcon.icns` has the same SHA-1 (`56683b5e…`) that was confirmed against Ali's artwork then. No new proposal.
