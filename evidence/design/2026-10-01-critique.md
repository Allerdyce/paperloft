# Paperloft Receipts: design critique, third review (AC-16), 2026-10-01

Reviewer: critic (Mac design lead role). Reviewed against the macOS Human Interface Guidelines and the six-line rubric. Earlier reviews: `evidence/design/2026-09-30-critique.md` and `evidence/design/2026-09-30-r2-critique.md`. I scored what I saw today, independently of those scores.

Build: QA build `build/QADerivedData/Build/Products/QA/Paperloft Receipts.app` (bundle id `app.paperloft.receipts`, version 1.1 (1), commit `93da86b`, not rebuilt).

Method: I looked only at the running app, with the background computer-use tools (screenshots, accessibility tree, clicks, background keystrokes, menu commands). I read no source code or tests. GUI lock `~/Factory/.gui.lock` was held as `critic3` from 23:55Z until 00:20Z, then removed. The app was quit at the end. The Mac's appearance is Light. I switched appearance with the app's own Settings › General › Theme control and left it on **System** in every profile I used.

Launches:

- **Launch A (first launch):** `-PaperloftQAProfile critic3-onboarding -PaperloftStoreMock Free -PaperloftScreenshotMode YES`. I reviewed onboarding, then pressed "Try with Samples". The samples were read with real-looking values (Maple Desk Supply and others). I filed, undid and filed again in this practice library.
- **Launch B (populated):** `-PaperloftUITestMode YES -PaperloftModel stub -PaperloftStoreMock Free -PaperloftScreenshotMode YES`. The Inbox was an empty practice library. Settings has no "Start Fresh Sample Library" or "New Sample Library" control in this build (AX search for "sample" found nothing). I relaunched with `-PaperloftQAProfile critic3-populated … -PaperloftQuotaUsed 25`, but UITestMode seems to ignore the QA profile: I got the same empty practice library. To get Inbox content I made two plain-text receipt PDFs in my scratchpad (`birch-hardware.pdf`, `lantern-books.pdf`) and sent them to the running app with `open -a <app> <file>`, which works like dropping a file on the Dock icon. That exercised the real limit-reached path. "Sample merchant" and the flagged Date and Total on lantern-books come from the stub reader, so they're expected test data.
- **Purchases:** I pressed the simulated Yearly purchase once (StoreMock). No system purchase sheet and no sign-in sheet appeared. I entered no password, Apple ID or payment detail.

Scores: 5 excellent, 4 good, 3 needs work, 2 poor, 1 broken. Every score below 4 has a fix.

## 1. Top findings

The second-review P1s are fixed. Undo from History now returns the receipt to the Inbox and offers Show in Inbox. Remove can be undone with Edit › Undo and has a notice banner. Confirm shows a notice and selects the next row. ↓ in the list keeps focus in the list. Inbox rows now lead with merchant and total. Quick Look shows the real file name. Library and History have honest empty states. The export sheet shows what it will include and warns about unfiled Inbox items. The review pane now behaves as intended: the preview stays in place while you move between documents, and only the fields column refreshes.

What still needs work, ranked by severity:

1. **P1: The review pane's layout is unstable.** I saw four related defects:
   - After a filing, the fields column narrows until another document reloads. Vendor goes from 208 to 139 px and Date from 160 to 91 px, and the preview widens to fill the space. I reproduced this twice, once with Return and once with Receipt › Confirm and File (`16`, `17`, `20`).
   - When the notice banner or the "Worth a check" note appears, the window content gets taller than the window. The heading band is squeezed, the heading is clipped at the top, and the "Return to confirm" hint is clipped at the bottom. Some of the squeeze stays after the banner closes (`41`, `42`, `43`).
   - Inbox rows overlap: the first row's "Issue" badge is cut off by the second row (`48`, `48b`).
   - One row's file name wrapped onto two lines, "2026-09-08_Juniper-/Cafe_19.98.pdf" (`18`). R2-06 says rows were made single-line because a row height that depended on pane width was the suspected cause of the AppKit "Update Constraints" crash, so this may be a crash risk, not only a visual defect.

   **Fix:**
   - Give the fields column a fixed ideal width (`.frame(minWidth: 320, idealWidth: 360)`) and keep the split position across reloads (`.navigationSplitViewColumnWidth` or an autosaved `NSSplitView`).
   - Put the fields in a `ScrollView`, with Remove and Confirm pinned in a bottom bar, so extra notes scroll rather than push the window.
   - Overlay the notice banner (`.overlay(alignment: .top)` or `.safeAreaInset(edge: .top)` on the content only) so it doesn't reflow the page.
   - Give every row `.lineLimit(1)` and `.truncationMode(.middle)` on every line, including the processing state and the "Returned by Undo" state, and use a fixed row height.
2. **P1: The paywall has no primary action and doesn't say why it appeared.**
   - It looks the same from Settings, from the export gate and when it opens by itself at the reading limit (`07`, `22`, `33`).
   - All five buttons are the same grey. The plan rows buy on a single click. A custom ✕ duplicates "Continue with Free", and "Reload Prices" is always shown.
   - After the simulated purchase, the sheet says "Paperloft Pro is active" but still shows the Free paragraph ("Free includes 25…") and the purchase buttons (`46`).
   - "No active purchases to restore." stays on screen into the next paywall and into Settings (`22`, `11`).

   **Fix:** see the paywall table (2.5). In short:
   - Use a radio-style plan picker with Yearly preselected and one prominent default button ("Start Free Trial" or "Buy Lifetime"). Put "Not Now" as the cancel button at the bottom right and drop the ✕.
   - Make the headline depend on the trigger. At the export gate: "Tax & Accountant Export is part of Pro". At the limit: "You've used this month's 25 automatic reads".
   - Give the Pro state its own content, and clear the restore message when the sheet closes.
   - StoreKit's `SubscriptionStoreView` or `StoreView` would give native layout, terms and restore for free.
3. **P2: Free-tier limit states are unclear.**
   - "Limit reached" uses the clock glyph and blue fill of "Processing" (`34b`).
   - A limit-reached item isn't counted in any filter chip (All 2, Issues 1, `37`/`44`).
   - The limit pane doesn't show the document or say when reads reset (`34`).
   - "Enter Details Myself" doesn't open blank fields: every field came back filled in, with Birch Hardware, $45.47 and $3.47 tax (`35`). The label promises manual entry, so users can't tell what was read for them.
   - Settings shows "25 of 25" in the same plain text as "0 of 25" (`32`).

   Fixes are in 2.2 and 2.4.
4. **P2: Undo brings a document back changed.** After Undo (History or Edit), the document comes back under its filed name ("2026-09-08_Juniper-Cafe_19.98.pdf"), at the bottom of the list, labelled "Returned by Undo". It goes through Processing again, and comes back with a different warning than before ("Verify total and receipt details" before, "Worth a check: …" after) (`18`, `19`, `41`). **Fix:** restore the original name, list position, values and flags exactly, with no re-read. Drop the "Returned by Undo" line, because the banner already says it.
5. **P2: The export sheet allows an empty export.** In Quarter Q4 it says "0 receipts in this period", but "Choose Destination & Export…" stays enabled and prominent (`51`). **Fix:** disable it and change the line to "No filed receipts in Q4 2026. Choose another period."
6. **P2: Dark mode selection contrast.** In dark mode the focused Inbox row is white text on #66AA7B, which is **2.76:1** (`15b`). **Fix:** use #2E6B47 (6.3:1 with white) or the system selection colour. Alternatively, keep the light green and use dark text #0F2A1C (5.6:1).
7. **P2: The notice banner has small defects.** It uses path syntax ("Filed to 2026/Meals/") while the form says "Files to 2026 › Meals" (`16`, `37`). Its dismiss button is a 10×10 pt target. It disappears in under 10 s, so I missed its Undo once; ⌘Z still worked. **Fix:** "Filed to Meals › 2026", a 20 pt minimum hit area, and keep the banner until the next action or 8 s, pausing while the pointer is over it.
8. **P2: Chrome and controls carried over from earlier reviews.** These are still open:
   - the inline Settings page in the sidebar
   - the tagline heading "A place for your paperwork"
   - the grey circle behind the sidebar toggle
   - the custom chips, checkboxes, date field and focus halo
   - the card-style Library with in-content search and the "View" pill
   - ASCII "..." on buttons
   - Help scrolling under the title bar

   The menu bar extra and the icon are covered in section 3 and section 5.

**Menu bar extra:** N/A. I found no status item, no Settings toggle and no window for one. Help still says "Drag … onto the window that opens from the menu bar item" (`23`); remove that sentence or ship the item.

**Icon:** confirmed. The QA build's tray now reads at 16 and 32 px on light and dark (`60`, `60b`). No new proposal.
