# Paperloft Receipts support

Questions or problems? Email <support@paperloft.app>; we read every message. If you're reporting a problem, include your Mac model and macOS version. Please don't send receipts or other documents unless we ask. Paperloft never needs them to help you.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. Which Macs does Paperloft Receipts need?
A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 27 or later. Understanding documents uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac. If it isn't available or turned on, Paperloft falls back to its built-in reader, and you check more fields yourself.

### 2. Does Paperloft upload my documents?
No. Everything happens on your Mac, and the app has no network access. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

### 3. Do I need an account?
No. There's nothing to sign up for and no API key to set up. Choose a library folder and start.

### 4. Where are my files kept, and what happens to the originals?
Filed documents are ordinary files in the library folder you choose, sorted into year and category folders and named like `2026-09-18_Office-Depot_74.90.pdf`. By default Paperloft files a copy and leaves your original in place. You can choose Move in Settings › Filing. Paperloft never overwrites or deletes a file.

### 5. Which documents can I add?
PDFs, including multi-page PDFs, and PNG, JPEG, HEIC and TIFF images, plus saved emails (`.eml`). For an email, Paperloft uses the receipt attachment. If there isn't one, it saves the email itself as a PDF for review.

### 6. How do I add documents?
Any of these:
- drag files onto the Paperloft window or its menu bar item
- choose File › Import Receipts…
- copy an image and choose Edit › Paste Image (⇧⌘V)
- in Finder, choose Share › Paperloft Receipts
- scan with an iPhone or iPad from File › Import from iPhone or iPad
- use the File Document action in Shortcuts

With Paperloft Pro, a watched folder (Settings › Watched folder) sends new files to your inbox automatically.

### 7. Why does a document say "Issue" or ask me to check a field?
Every document waits in the Review inbox for your confirmation; nothing is filed without it. Paperloft highlights fields it couldn't read confidently, totals it couldn't verify against the document, and documents whose type it couldn't determine. Correct anything that's wrong, then press Return to file. "Not a receipt" and Remove set a document aside without filing it.

### 8. Can I undo a filing?
Yes. Every filing batch is listed in History and can be undone. Edit › Undo Last Filing (⌘Z) undoes the most recent one. Undo restores files to where they were, including originals you chose to move.

### 9. How do I give my accountant everything for the year?
In Library, choose Tax & Accountant Export…, pick a year, quarter or custom range, and choose where to save it. The accountant pack contains a summary PDF, a CSV of transactions and the documents sorted by category, optionally as a ZIP. From Shortcuts, Export Accountant Pack (Paperloft Pro) returns the ZIP for packs up to 100 MB. Export larger packs from the app.

### 10. Is Paperloft tax advice?
No. Paperloft organises your records into editable categories. It doesn't judge what's deductible or give tax advice. Ask your accountant.
